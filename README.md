# shorttimegoldprice
Prediction of price of gold for the next twenty four hours
Prices of gold are important as it serves as a safe haven for investors and backs up currencies. Yet its randomness and volatility limits accurate prediction using traditional models
The task is to use ML and DL models for comparative study to get improved accuracy in gold price predition
Random Forest (ML) and LSTM (ML) models were used to fit OHLCV 1-hour time frame data for gold (obtained from yahoo finance) in the previous 60 days, and the best of the two models is chosen based on accurcy and used to predict price of gold for the next 24 hours
The price of gold was modelled with a fit of over 99% based on R-squared and the price of gold in the next 24 hours predicted to a high level of accuracy
